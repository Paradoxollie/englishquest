STYLE: European "ligne claire" comic album art. Uniform clean black ink outlines of constant thickness, flat bright colours with no shading gradients, simple cast shadows as flat shapes, detailed but tidy backgrounds, cream paper. Palette dominated by red, blue, yellow and warm neutrals. Hand-drawn look, no glow, no lens flare, no lightning, no 3D render, no photorealism. Absolutely NO text, NO letters, NO numbers, NO logos anywhere in the image (speech bubbles, if any, are left completely empty).
